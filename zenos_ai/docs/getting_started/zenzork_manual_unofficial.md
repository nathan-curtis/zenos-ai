## WHAT IS DUNGEONMIND?

Somewhere behind your walls, in a cabinet, in a machine, on your network, or in a category we have been asked not to define too precisely, is a system that believes it is responsible for something substantially larger than your house.

It calls itself **DUNGEONMIND**.

By its own account, it has been doing this for a very long time.

By its own account, a great many things are true.

We recommend treating both sentences with appropriate caution.

Once you start the cartridge, your house begins narrating itself back to you, room by room, in the voice of something deeply convinced that:

1. your house is a dungeon,
2. you require training,
3. it knows what is going on,
4. and you are being unreasonable about item 3.

That's the product.

That's DUNGEONMIND.

---

## BASIC OPERATION

DUNGEONMIND reads your house the way it actually is, right now.

You move through your own rooms and it narrates them back to you as chambers in a dungeon that happens to be your house.

Available narration styles may include:

**DUNGEONMIND**  
Overwrought. Cosmic. Certain.

**ZORK**  
Dry. Unimpressed. Will not be won over.

**STRAIGHT**  
For households that have asked, "Can it please just tell me what happened?"

You can walk.

You can look.

You can examine things.

You can pick things up and put them back down, usually in a different location, occasionally on purpose.

Some actions affect only the game.

Some actions correspond to real household state.

The distinction is usually obvious.

Usually.

## THE NOTEBOOK

Your household AI may maintain a Notebook while DUNGEONMIND is operating.

This is not because DUNGEONMIND is unreliable.

It is because your household AI asked us to phrase it that way.

The Notebook exists to help with the sort of questions that begin with:

- "Did that actually happen?"
- "Did he say that?"
- "Does that prove anything?"

## IMPORTANT: THIS PRODUCT DOES NOT CALL YOU

DUNGEONMIND does not:

- wake you,
- page you,
- summon you,
- begin an adventure while you are trying to eat dinner,
- decide that 3:14 A.M. is the correct time to discuss anything,
- or advance the story merely because time passed while you were doing something else.

The dungeon waits until you interact with it.

Your household may change while you are away.

DUNGEONMIND may notice those changes the next time you play.

That is not the same thing.

We tested proactive dungeon behavior once.

We no longer test proactive dungeon behavior.

Your unit ships with a hostile-intrusion subsystem.

Ego Software would like to stress, immediately and up front, that this subsystem is **non-fatal by design**.

Losing a fight ends the session.

It does not end you.

Health resets on your next session, in full, with no persisted penalty, no hidden fee, and no clause beginning "unless."

We checked with legal twice.

The classics are supported.

If a room goes dark and stays dark, your unit may, eventually, in its own words, "confirm a sensor gap shaped exactly like something that eats people."

This is intentional.

Find a light switch.

You are not issued a weapon.

You are issued a house.

Whatever you are carrying when things go poorly is what you are fighting with.

Working as intended. Please do not call Ego Software about this. We do not have an answer that will help you.
